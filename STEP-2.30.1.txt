FC EDINET v2.30.1 FULL - FAN DASHBOARD AND FAVORITES

INSTALLATION
1. Extract the ZIP to E:\Projects\FC Edinet\fc-edinet-v2.30.1 alongside the prior release.
2. Run DEPLOY_FC_EDINET.bat. Do not manually copy environment secrets into the ZIP.
3. Wait for all deployment checks and the real Next.js production build to complete.
4. Verify https://fc-edinet.vercel.app/api/version reports 2.30.1.
5. In PowerShell inside the project folder: npm run qa:live

FAN ACCOUNT ACCEPTANCE
1. Log in with an existing user account and open /account.
2. Add a future match to favorites from /matches; verify it appears as the next saved match in /account.
3. Download its .ics calendar entry and check the date in your calendar app.
4. Save one completed match and verify it appears in recent favorite results (with the real result, including 0:0 if appropriate).
5. Save one player, then open their profile from the personal dashboard.
6. Open /account/favorites and switch All / Upcoming / Results; verify proper ordering and removing a favorite.
7. Repeat on a phone, check both RU and RO, and confirm no horizontal page scrolling.
8. Log out and confirm private /account content requires login.

No Supabase migration; existing favorite records are preserved. The account dashboard does NOT send email or push notifications.
