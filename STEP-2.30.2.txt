FC EDINET v2.30.2 FULL — MATCH PHOTO ALBUMS

1. Run database/074_media_match_albums.sql ONCE in Supabase SQL Editor.
2. Deploy using DEPLOY_FC_EDINET.bat from this version folder.
3. Admin > Media: create/edit an album and select a match (optional).
4. Publish album, upload photos, and open /matches/<match_id>.
5. Scroll to Photo Gallery / Fotografii de la meci and open the album.
6. Confirm RU/RO and mobile behavior.

IMPORTANT: The Supabase migration must precede editing albums in v2.30.2.
This release does not delete previous albums or photos.
