FC EDINEȚ v2.9.7 — Player Statistics Completion Fix

Fixes:
- calendar label rename is confirmed unrelated to player statistics
- fixed regression introduced when the separate Complete button was removed
- Save now automatically marks player statistics COMPLETE for a finished match when:
  * 11 starters are saved
  * at least one starting goalkeeper exists
  * the competition is linked to a season
- event-derived player_match_stats continue to sync on save/create/update/delete event
- publishing a protocol performs an extra statistics sync and completes valid player statistics as a safety net
- season/tournament totals (appearances, minutes, goals, assists, yellow/red cards) become visible again

Recovery for matches saved in v2.9.5-v2.9.6:
Open the affected finished match in Match Center and press Save once.

No SQL migration required.
